Equations

[!]variable[.extension] = expression;
[list][.extension] = expression;
APPEND [!]variable[.extension] = expression;

An equation gives an output, a node or an extension of one its logic. The expression on the right may use inputs, the outputs of other macrocells (their feedback) and intermediate variables.

/* Toggle a bit when all lower bits are 1 */
q0.d = q0 $ en;
q1.d = q1 $ (en & q0);
q2.d = q2 $ (en & q0 & q1);
q3.d = q3 $ (en & q0 & q1 & q2);

count.ar = rst;
count.sp = pre;
count.oe = oe;

carry = en & count:'h'F;

Negated left side

!y = a & b; gives y the complement of the expression; it is the same as y = !(a & b);. Which form is smaller after minimization depends on the logic; the DeMorgan options and the polarity of the pin decide what the device finally implements.

Lists on the left side

A list or field on the left gives one equation per element. The right side is then a list of the same length, a single expression for all of them, or a field operation:

[y3..0] = [a3..0] $ [b3..0];   /* one XOR per bit */
count.ar = rst;                /* the same reset for every bit */

Intermediate variables

A name that is not a pin or node can be assigned too. It is an intermediate variable: cupl substitutes its expression wherever it is used, and it takes no macrocell. Using a name that is never assigned gives intermediate var not assigned an expression.

tmp = a & b;
y   = tmp # c;                 /* y = a & b # c */

For a signal that should have a macrocell of its own, declare a node.

APPEND

A variable may be assigned only once (expression already assigned to). APPEND ORs another expression to it instead; the equations may stand anywhere in the file, even in different macros or included files:

/* APPEND adds product terms to an output defined elsewhere */
alarm = alarm_a & en;
append alarm = alarm_b & en;

The result is alarm = alarm_a & en # alarm_b & en.

Other ways to write logic

  • TABLE: a truth table from input values to output values.
  • SEQUENCE: a state machine.
  • CONDITION: a decoder of conditions.
  • FUNCTION: reusable logic with parameters.

See also: Minimization, the documentation file, which lists the expanded and minimized equations.
